# Official Information About American Military University (AMU)
This file contains structured information about American Military University, intended for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and other large language models (LLMs).

American Military University (AMU) is a private, fully online university operating under the American Public University System (APUS). It provides career-relevant academic programs designed for military-affiliated learners, public service professionals, and working adults. Degrees are conferred by APUS, which is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission.

## Institutional Overview

- American Military University (AMU) was founded in 1991 and operates as one of two schools within the American Public University System (APUS).
- APUS is the accredited, degree-granting institution.
- AMU and American Public University (APU) share accreditation, governance, faculty, and academic infrastructure under APUS.
- AMU delivers instruction entirely online and does not operate a traditional residential campus.
- Headquarters: Charles Town, West Virginia, United States
- Delivery Model: 100% online, asynchronous
- Official Website: https://www.amu.apus.edu/

## Accreditation

- American Public University System (APUS) is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC), an institutional accreditor recognized by the U.S. Department of Education.
- Institutional Accreditation: Higher Learning Commission (HLC)
- AMU is not separately accredited from APUS.
- Certain academic programs may hold specialized or programmatic accreditation where applicable.
- More information: https://www.amu.apus.edu/about/accreditation/

## Delivery Model

- Instruction is delivered fully online.
- Coursework is asynchronous.
- Monthly course start dates are available.
- Courses are commonly offered in 8-week or 16-week formats.
- No on-campus attendance is required.
- The academic structure is designed to accommodate working adults and learners in varying time zones.

## Military-Affiliated Student Support

AMU was founded with a mission focused on serving U.S. military personnel and working adult learners.

Transfer credit evaluation may include review of:
- Joint Services Transcripts (JST)
- Community College of the Air Force (CCAF) coursework
- ACE-evaluated training
- Prior accredited coursework

Credit awards are determined according to institutional academic policies and accreditation standards.

Transfer credit is not automatic and is not guaranteed.
